ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

A forum for discovery, collaboration and innovation

International Conference on Microbiological Sciences (ICMS–2027) is a three-day academic gathering organised by the Department of Microbiology, Dr. Rammanohar Lohia Avadh University, Ayodhya, in association with Microbiologists Society, India. The conference will bring together researchers, faculty members, scientists, students and professionals to exchange ideas on current developments in microbiology and biotechnology.

With the theme “Innovations in Microbiology & Biotechnology for a Sustainable Future,” the conference will provide a platform to discuss how microbial science can contribute to human and animal health, environmental protection, agriculture, food systems, industrial processes and emerging biotechnologies.

Through scientific presentations and professional interaction, ICMS–2027 aims to encourage the sharing of research findings, interdisciplinary discussion and new academic connections across institutions and research areas.

EXPLORE THE SCIENCE

Scientific themes

ICMS–2027 welcomes original research and ideas spanning fundamental, applied and translational microbiology.

01

Medical & Clinical Microbiology

Pathogens, diagnostics, infection biology and emerging infectious diseases.

02

Antimicrobial Resistance & One Health

AMR surveillance, antimicrobial stewardship and interconnected human, animal and environmental health.

03

Environmental & Agricultural Microbiology

Microbial ecology, soil and water systems, agriculture and environmental sustainability.

04

Microbial Biotechnology & Nanobiotechnology

Microbial products, bioprocesses, bioremediation and emerging biotechnological applications.

05

Genomics, Metagenomics & Bioinformatics

Sequencing, microbial diversity, computational biology and microbiome research.

06

Virology, Food & Industrial Microbiology

Viruses and bacteriophages, food safety, fermentation and industrial applications.
