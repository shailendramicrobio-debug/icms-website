Plan ahead

Useful booking advice

Before confirming a hotel

  • January is a busy travel period in Ayodhya; reserve rooms well in advance.
  • Confirm tariffs, taxes, availability and cancellation policies directly with the property or booking platform.
  • Ask whether a conference rate is available and mention ICMS–2027 when contacting hotels.

Please note

  • University guest-house accommodation, if requested, requires prior approval and is subject to availability.
  • The conference organisers cannot guarantee availability, pricing or service quality at third-party accommodation providers.
  • Hotel distances and online listings are indicative and should be reconfirmed before travel.
