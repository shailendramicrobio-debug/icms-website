Accommodation

Accommodation information for delegates attending the International Conference on Microbiological Sciences, 07–09 January 2027, Ayodhya.

Stay in Ayodhya

Accommodation Options

Delegates may choose from university accommodation, premium hotels, business and mid-range hotels, guest houses and homestays in and around Ayodhya/Faizabad. Availability and tariffs may vary during the conference period, so early booking is strongly recommended.

University Guest House

Limited accommodation may be available through the university, subject to institutional availability and prior confirmation.

Recommended for: invited speakers, officials and delegates requiring campus proximity.

Premium Hotels

Higher-end properties are available in Ayodhya and Faizabad for delegates seeking enhanced facilities.

Business & Mid-Range Hotels

Several hotels are located within a short drive of the conference venue and provide practical options for academic and professional delegates.

Budget Hotels & Homestays

Budget hotels, guest houses and homestays provide economical accommodation options for students and research scholars.

Booking Advice

Important Information for Delegates

  • January is a busy travel period in Ayodhya; delegates are advised to reserve rooms well in advance.
  • Hotel tariffs, room availability, taxes and cancellation policies can change. Confirm final terms directly with the property or booking platform.
  • Delegates may mention ICMS–2027 when contacting hotels and ask whether a conference rate is available.
  • University guest-house accommodation, if requested, is subject to availability and prior approval.
  • The conference organisers do not guarantee the availability, pricing or service quality of third-party accommodation providers.
