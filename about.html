The scientific themes of the International Conference on Microbiological Sciences (ICMS–2027) are designed to highlight recent advances, emerging challenges, and innovative applications of microbiology and biotechnology for a sustainable future. The conference will provide a multidisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, and students to exchange knowledge and discuss developments. These themes aim to promote scientific collaboration, encourage innovative research, address global health and environmental challenges, and explore the potential of microbial sciences in sustainable agriculture, biotechnology, disease prevention, and environmental protection. Through interdisciplinary discussions and knowledge sharing, ICMS–2027 seeks to strengthen the role of microbiology and biotechnology in building a healthier, safer, and more sustainable future.

SCIENTIFIC THEMES

Explore the frontiers of microbiology.

Medical and Clinical Microbiology

Pathogens, diagnostics, clinical microbiology, infection biology and emerging infectious diseases.

Antimicrobial Resistance and Infectious Diseases

AMR surveillance, resistance mechanisms, antimicrobial stewardship and infectious disease research.

Environmental and Agricultural Microbiology

Microbial ecology, soil and water microbiology, plant–microbe interactions and sustainable agriculture.

Food and Industrial Microbiology

Food safety, probiotics, fermentation, industrial microorganisms, enzymes and microbial products.

Microbial Biotechnology and Nanobiotechnology

Microbial bioprocesses, nanomaterials, bioinnovation, synthetic biology and advanced applications.

Genomics, Metagenomics and Bioinformatics

Genome analysis, metagenomics, microbial diversity, sequencing, computational biology and data science.

Microbiome Research and Microbial Enology

Microbiome structure and function, host–microbe interactions, fermentation and microbial enology.

Virology and Bacteriophages

Viral biology, emerging viruses, phage biology, phage therapy and virus–host interactions.

One Health and Wastewater Surveillance

Integrated human–animal–environment health, wastewater epidemiology and microbial surveillance.

Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies in Microbiology

AI/ML, automation, advanced diagnostics, imaging, biosensors and emerging technologies in microbiology.

HOST INSTITUTION

About the Organizing University

Dr. Rammanohar Lohia Avadh University (RMLAU), commonly known as Avadh University, is a state university located in Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh. Established on 4 March 1975 by the Government of Uttar Pradesh as Avadh University, it was renamed in 1993–94 in memory of Dr. Rammanohar Lohia, a noted socio-economic ideologue and freedom fighter.

The university has grown into a major higher-education institution, currently serving about 5 lakh regular and private students through nine residential postgraduate departments, an on-campus Institute of Engineering & Technology, and over 600 affiliated colleges across multiple districts in the Avadh region. Spread across 112.24 acres, the campus houses 23 academic and administrative buildings, including the central library, Swami Vivekananda auditorium, examination centre, IT centre, hostels, and specialized departmental facilities.

RMLAU offers a diverse range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, science, humanities, management, biotechnology, mass communication, and more, supported by departments such as Physics & Electronics, Mathematics & Statistics, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Environmental Sciences, and Economics & Rural Development. The university is affiliated with the UGC and holds a NAAC B accreditation, reflecting its commitment to academic quality and regional educational development.

With its motto “Dedicated to Truth”, RMLAU continues to advance research, professional education, and cultural integration through initiatives like the Sardar Patel Centre for National Integration, established in 2020 to promote interdisciplinary studies in governance, strategic affairs, and international relations.

About the Microbiologists Society, India

The Microbiologists Society, India (MBSI) is a national scientific organization founded in 1996 that advances microbiology through education, research, outreach, and nationwide student engagement.

Origins and Leadership

Dr. Arvind M. Deshmukh, a distinguished microbiologist with over 37 years of teaching and research experience, founded MBSI in 1996. He previously served as Professor and Head of Microbiology at Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Marathwada University’s Osmanabad campus.

Under his leadership, the society has grown into one of India’s active microbiology networks, organizing state, national, and international programs across India, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Dubai, Mauritius, Bangladesh, Nepal, Maldives, and the USA.

For more details: microbiosociety.com
