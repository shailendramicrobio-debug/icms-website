ABSTRACT SUBMISSION

Guidelines for Abstract Submission

International Conference on Microbiological Sciences (ICMS–2027)
07–09 January 2027 | Department of Microbiology, Dr. Rammanohar Lohia Avadh University, Ayodhya

01

General Guidelines

  • Abstracts must present original research work that has not been published or presented elsewhere.
  • Abstracts should be relevant to the theme or scientific tracks of ICMS–2027.
  • Each participant may submit an abstract for oral or poster presentation.
  • The abstract should be written in clear, concise English.
  • Maximum length: 300 words, excluding the title, authors, affiliations, and keywords.
  • Avoid unnecessary references, tables, figures, and citations in the abstract.
02

Abstract Format

Please prepare the abstract in the following order:

Title

  • Concise, informative, and preferably not exceeding 20 words.
  • Use Title Case and avoid unnecessary abbreviations.

Authors

  • Full name of all authors.
  • Underline the presenting author.
  • Use superscript numbers to indicate affiliations.

Affiliation

  • Department/Institute/University
  • City, State, Country
  • E-mail address of the corresponding author

Keywords

Provide 3–5 keywords below the abstract.

SUGGESTED ABSTRACT TEMPLATE

Use the following structure when preparing your abstract

TITLE OF THE ABSTRACT

Author A1*, Author B2 and Author C1

FULL-LENGTH MANUSCRIPT SUBMISSION

Call for Full Research Paper for publication in a Scopus-indexed journal

Authors who present their research at ICMS–2027 are invited to submit full-length manuscripts for consideration for publication in a suitable Scopus-indexed journal.

General note: Only original, unpublished research manuscripts relevant to the conference themes should be submitted. All manuscripts will be subject to editorial screening, peer review, journal scope and quality requirements, and the independent decision of the journal. Conference presentation or submission does not guarantee acceptance or publication. Authors are responsible for checking journal policies, publication charges, ethics approvals, and any required declarations. Scopus coverage and publication arrangements will be verified before any journal is announced as a publication partner.
