ABSTRACT SUBMISSION

Guidelines for Abstract Submission

International Conference on Microbiological Sciences (ICMS–2027)
07–09 January 2027 | Department of Microbiology, Dr. Rammanohar Lohia Avadh University, Ayodhya

01

General Guidelines

  • Abstracts must present original research work that has not been published or presented elsewhere.
  • Abstracts should be relevant to the theme or scientific tracks of ICMS–2027.
  • Each participant may submit an abstract for oral or poster presentation.
  • The abstract should be written in clear, concise English.
  • Maximum length: 300 words, excluding the title, authors, affiliations, and keywords.
  • Avoid unnecessary references, tables, figures, and citations in the abstract.
02

Abstract Format

Please prepare the abstract in the following order:

Title

  • Concise, informative, and preferably not exceeding 20 words.
  • Use Title Case and avoid unnecessary abbreviations.

Authors

  • Full name of all authors.
  • Underline the presenting author.
  • Use superscript numbers to indicate affiliations.

Affiliation

  • Department/Institute/University
  • City, State, Country
  • E-mail address of the corresponding author

Keywords

Provide 3–5 keywords below the abstract.

SUGGESTED ABSTRACT TEMPLATE

Use the following structure when preparing your abstract

TITLE OF THE ABSTRACT

Author A1*, Author B2 and Author C1

1Department of Microbiology, XYZ University, India
2Department of Biotechnology, ABC Institute, India
*Corresponding author: email@example.com

Abstract: [Write the abstract within 300 words, preferably covering Background/Objectives, Methods, Results and Conclusion.]

Keywords: Antimicrobial resistance; Microbial biotechnology; Metagenomics; One Health; Microbiology

CALL FOR FULL-LENGTH RESEARCH PAPERS

Publication Opportunity for ICMS–2027

Invitation to Submit Original Full-Length Research Papers

Participants of the International Conference on Microbiological Sciences (ICMS–2027) are invited to submit original, full-length research papers based on their scientific work relevant to the conference theme, “Innovations in Microbiology & Biotechnology for a Sustainable Future.”

The broad areas include microbiology, biotechnology, and sustainability, including medical and clinical microbiology; antimicrobial resistance and infectious diseases; environmental and agricultural microbiology; food and industrial microbiology; microbial biotechnology and nanobiotechnology; genomics, metagenomics and bioinformatics; microbiome research; virology and bacteriophages; One Health and wastewater surveillance; and artificial intelligence and emerging technologies in microbiology.

Selected manuscripts may be considered for publication in a special volume or issue of a suitable Scopus-indexed journal, subject to journal scope, editorial screening, peer review, acceptance by the journal, and compliance with its publication policies. Submission or presentation at the conference does not guarantee publication.

Submission guidance

  • Submit only original work that is not published or under consideration elsewhere.
  • Prepare the manuscript according to the target journal’s author guidelines and ethical requirements.
  • All manuscripts will be subject to the journal’s independent editorial assessment and peer-review process.
  • Publication timelines, journal details, submission procedures, and applicable publication charges will be communicated when confirmed by the organizers and the journal.

Important: Please await the official announcement for the selected journal(s), submission deadline, and manuscript submission instructions. Do not submit a manuscript directly to a journal as an ICMS–2027 paper until the relevant arrangements have been confirmed.

AWARDS & RECOGNITION

Awards at ICMS–2027

ICMS–2027 will recognize outstanding scientific presentations and promising research contributions. Participants interested in an award should indicate their interest when submitting their abstract, subject to the final instructions issued by the Organizing Committee.

01

Best Oral Presentation Award

For an outstanding oral presentation demonstrating scientific merit, clear communication, and effective interpretation of research findings.

Evaluation may consider:

  • Originality and significance of the research
  • Soundness of methods and interpretation of results
  • Clarity, organization, and delivery of the presentation
  • Responses to questions from the evaluation panel
02

Best Poster Presentation Award

For a poster that communicates important research findings effectively through a clear visual layout and an informed discussion with evaluators.

Evaluation may consider:

  • Scientific quality, originality, and relevance
  • Organization, readability, and visual presentation
  • Clarity of explanation and discussion
  • Ability to answer questions about the work
03

Young Scientist Award

Intended to recognize promising early-career researchers presenting high-quality original research at ICMS–2027.

Eligibility: Applicants should submit original research and be the presenting author. The age limit, required proof of age, career-stage conditions, and any additional eligibility requirements will be announced by the Organizing Committee.

Award Participation and Presentation Guidelines

  • Submit an original abstract relevant to the conference theme or scientific tracks, following the abstract format and 300-word limit given above.
  • Clearly indicate the preferred presentation category (oral or poster) and the award category for which you wish to be considered, where applicable.
  • The presenting author should be registered for the conference and available to present the work and answer questions during the assigned session.
  • Presentations will be evaluated by a committee appointed by the Organizing Committee. The evaluation criteria listed above are indicative and may be supplemented by the committee.
  • Final eligibility rules, presentation formats, evaluation procedures, and award details will be communicated in the conference circulars and programme.

Important: Please await the official announcement for the Young Scientist Award age limit and supporting-document requirements before submitting an application under that category.
